From Student to Professional with Animora

Animora is built to guide artists through education, income, and career growth — not just creativity.

Year 1 — Foundation

  • Learn drawing fundamentals and visual storytelling
  • Use AI feedback to improve daily
  • Create small artworks and social posts
  • Offline work supported, upload later

Year 2 — Skill Building & Exposure

  • Explore animation basics and storyboards
  • Follow art trends using Animora Trend Feed
  • Start freelancing small gigs
  • Build a digital portfolio

Year 3 — Monetization & Identity

  • Create assets, templates, and short animations
  • Sell artwork or content to clients
  • Use captions & trend tools for growth
  • Prepare for internships or studio work

Year 4 — Professional Transition

  • Launch a personal brand or studio path
  • Work with clients or production teams
  • Earn sustainably from creative skills
  • Graduate with real experience, not just a degree

Animora ensures creativity becomes a career — not a gamble.
